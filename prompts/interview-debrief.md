# Interview Debrief Synthesis

## Purpose

Organize interviewer feedback without allowing AI to make the hiring decision.

## Prompt

```text
Organize the sanitized interview feedback below into a structured debrief.

For each competency:
1. Summarize the evidence provided.
2. Separate observed evidence from interviewer interpretation.
3. Identify conflicting feedback.
4. Flag vague statements or conclusions without evidence.
5. List unanswered questions.

Then provide:
- Evidence supported consistently across interviews
- Material concerns supported by evidence
- Information gaps requiring follow-up

Rules:
- Do not recommend hire or no-hire.
- Do not infer protected characteristics or personality traits.
- Do not add facts that are not in the feedback.
- Do not treat repeated opinions as independent evidence.
- Quote only brief phrases when necessary.

COMPETENCIES:
[Paste defined competencies]

SANITIZED INTERVIEW FEEDBACK:
[Paste feedback]
```

## Human review

The interview team owns the decision. Confirm that feedback is job-related, evidence-based, and evaluated against the same predefined criteria for every candidate.
