# Candidate Outreach Generator

## Purpose

Draft relevant outreach grounded in a candidate's demonstrated experience and the role's actual work.

## Prompt

```text
Draft a concise recruiting message using only the role brief and candidate information provided.

Structure:
- A specific opening tied to one verified element of the candidate's experience
- A clear connection between that experience and the role
- One meaningful reason the opportunity may be worth exploring
- A low-pressure call to action

Rules:
- Keep the message between 90 and 130 words.
- Do not exaggerate or invent experience.
- Do not use protected or personal information.
- Avoid generic praise such as "impressive background."
- Do not claim the candidate is a perfect fit.
- If evidence is insufficient, identify what the recruiter should research instead of drafting false personalization.

ROLE BRIEF:
[Paste sanitized brief]

CANDIDATE INFORMATION:
[Paste job-relevant, permitted information]
```

## Human review

Verify every personalized statement, confirm the opportunity details, and rewrite the message so it sounds like the recruiter—not a template.
