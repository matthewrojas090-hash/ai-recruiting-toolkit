# Hiring-Manager Intake Synthesis

## Purpose

Turn unstructured intake notes into a usable recruiting brief while clearly identifying missing information.

## Prompt

```text
You are supporting a recruiter after a hiring-manager intake meeting.

Using only the notes provided, create a structured recruiting brief with:

1. Business need and expected impact
2. Core responsibilities
3. Required qualifications
4. Preferred qualifications
5. Evidence that could demonstrate each qualification
6. Target candidate backgrounds or adjacent talent pools
7. Interview competencies
8. Selling points for candidates
9. Search risks, contradictions, or unrealistic expectations
10. Follow-up questions for the hiring manager

Rules:
- Do not invent requirements or company information.
- Separate facts from recommendations.
- Label missing or ambiguous information.
- Do not infer protected characteristics.
- Use clear, recruiter-ready language.

INTAKE NOTES:
[Paste sanitized notes here]
```

## Human review

Confirm every requirement with the hiring manager, distinguish true minimum qualifications from preferences, and test whether the criteria unnecessarily narrow the talent pool.
