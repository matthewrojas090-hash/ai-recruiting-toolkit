# Responsible AI Checklist for Recruiting

Use this checklist before adopting an AI-assisted recruiting workflow.

## Data protection

- Remove candidate names and unnecessary personally identifiable information.
- Do not enter confidential employer, compensation, medical, immigration, or background-check information into unapproved tools.
- Follow company retention, access, and security requirements.
- Use synthetic data when testing a workflow.

## Accuracy

- Require the system to distinguish facts, assumptions, and recommendations.
- Verify claims against the original source.
- Reject fabricated qualifications, metrics, employers, or candidate motivations.
- Keep a human accountable for the final output.

## Fairness

- Do not infer race, ethnicity, age, disability, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, family status, or other protected information.
- Do not use AI to determine “culture fit.”
- Evaluate candidates against predefined, job-related competencies.
- Review whether criteria create unnecessary barriers or proxy discrimination.

## Candidate experience

- Ensure AI-assisted communication remains accurate and respectful.
- Give candidates meaningful access to a human.
- Do not misrepresent an automated interaction as personal research.
- Protect candidate confidentiality throughout the process.

## Decision boundary

AI may help organize, summarize, draft, and identify missing information. It should not independently reject candidates or make final employment decisions.

## Before launch

- Obtain approval from Legal, Privacy, Security, and HR as required.
- Document the workflow, data sources, reviewer, and escalation path.
- Test for accuracy and disparate impact.
- Reevaluate the workflow regularly.
